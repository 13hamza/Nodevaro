About Nodevaro

Three specialities, one small team, no handoffs lost in translation.

Nodevaro exists because we kept seeing the same problem: a business hires a web developer, then an automation freelancer, then a marketing agency — and none of the three know what the others built.

How we got here

We started as separate contractors — one building websites, one building n8n workflows, one running paid campaigns — and kept getting brought onto the same projects to clean up after each other. A site launched with no way to capture leads. A workflow built with nowhere for its output to go. A campaign driving traffic to a form nobody was checking.

Nodevaro is what happened when we stopped taking those jobs separately and started taking them together. The name comes from the "nodes" that connect a workflow — which is exactly what the three of us do for a business: build the working connections between the parts that used to be handled in isolation.

How we work

We keep the team small on purpose. Every client talks directly to the person doing the work — there's no account manager relaying requests between you and a developer three time zones away.

That also means we're selective about what we take on. We'd rather do fewer projects well and stay reachable than scale into the kind of agency we used to get called in to fix.

What we value

Four things that show up in how a project actually runs.

Plain language

No jargon dressed up to sound impressive. If we can't explain what something does in one sentence, we haven't finished designing it.

Working software over decks

We'd rather show you a working prototype in week one than a polished slide deck. You should see progress, not promises.

Own the outcome

If an automation breaks or a page slows down, that's on us to catch — not something you should have to notice first.

Right-sized, not maximal

A five-person business doesn't need enterprise tooling. We scope for what you actually need to run and grow.

Want to see if we're a fit?

A 20-minute call tells us both, before either of us commits to anything.
