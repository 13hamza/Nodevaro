Services

Everything between "we need a website" and "leads follow themselves up."

Pick one service, or let all four hand off to each other. Each section below covers what's included, and what a typical engagement looks like.

01 / 04
Web & App Development

Websites and Android apps built to load fast, rank, and actually get maintained.

We design and build marketing sites, booking/portal-style web apps, and small e-commerce stores. Every project starts from your content and goals, not a stock template and ships with clean, documented code so you're never locked to us to make a text change.

Need a mobile presence too? We build Android apps using React Native, sharing logic and design language with the web build so both stay consistent as your business grows. Existing site already doing okay? We also take on redesigns, speed fixes, and ongoing maintenance for sites we didn't originally build.

  • Custom design in Figma, reviewed with you before any code is written
  • Responsive build (mobile, tablet, desktop) with accessible markup
  • Android app development in React Native
  • On-page SEO basics: page speed, meta tags, sitemap, structured data
  • Content editing you can do yourself, or a CMS if you'd rather not
  • 30 days of post-launch support included

02 / 04
Automation & AI

n8n workflows and AI assistants that handle the repetitive parts of your business.

n8n lets us connect the tools you already run your inbox, CRM, spreadsheets, calendar, WhatsApp or Slack into workflows that move information between them without anyone copy-pasting. On top of that, we build AI assistants that read, reply, and route: qualifying a lead, answering a common support question, or drafting a first-pass response for a human to approve.

For example, we've built fully automated AI virtual assistants for beauty clinic clients that handle appointment booking, answer customer queries, and send workflow notifications end to end no staff member manually confirming a slot. We start with the one or two workflows costing you the most hours, ship those, and expand from there so you see it working before committing to a bigger build.

  • Lead capture, qualification, and CRM sync from your website forms
  • AI chat & booking assistant for FAQs, appointments, or first-line support
  • Automated follow-ups, reminders, and invoice/payment nudges
  • Internal reporting: data pulled and formatted automatically, on schedule
  • Monitoring so a failed step alerts you instead of silently dropping data
Automate a workflow
03 / 04
Power BI & Reporting

Dashboards that turn raw operational data into decisions an owner can act on.

Most small businesses generate more data than anyone has time to read bookings, sales, stock, spreadsheets exported from three different tools. We design and deliver Power BI dashboards and reporting solutions that pull that raw data together and turn it into a small set of numbers worth checking.

We build for owners with no in-house analytics capability: the dashboard is designed to be read in two minutes, not interpreted by a data team, and updates on a schedule so you're never looking at last month's numbers.

  • Power BI dashboards built around the metrics that actually drive decisions
  • Data connected from your existing spreadsheets, CRM, or booking system
  • Clear visuals designed for owners, not analysts
  • Scheduled refreshes so reports stay current automatically
  • A short walkthrough so you know how to read it yourself

Digital Marketing

Marketing aimed at the site and automations we just built you.

Traffic is only useful if something on the other end can act on it so our marketing work is designed to feed the forms, chat assistants, and follow-up workflows we set up. That might mean SEO content, a managed social presence, or paid campaigns, depending on where your customers actually are.

You'll get a plain-language monthly report: what ran, what it cost, and what came out the other end not a wall of vanity metrics.

  • SEO: keyword targeting, content briefs, and technical fixes
  • Social media management and content calendar
  • Paid ads on Google and Meta, budgeted and optimised monthly
  • Email marketing sequences tied into your automations
  • Monthly reporting in plain language, not a raw analytics export
