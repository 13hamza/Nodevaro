Solar panel installation for small homes.

Alongside our web, data, automation, and marketing work, Nodevaro now designs and installs solar panel systems sized for small residential homes using panels imported from China. We currently install in Gujranwala only, with more cities planned as the team grows.

Get a free site estimate

What a small home system includes

Most small homes in Gujranwala run comfortably on a system sized to cover fans, lights, a fridge, and a few essential appliances, with room to add a washing machine or extra load later. We size the system to your actual bill and roof space, not a one-size-fits-all package.

Every installation includes the panels, mounting structure, inverter, wiring and protection gear, and net-metering paperwork if your home qualifies for it so you're not left chasing separate vendors for each piece.

How the process works

  • Free roof & load survey we visit your home and check your recent bills
  • A fixed-scope quote sized to your usage, not a generic package
  • Panels & inverter sourced and imported, structure fabricated to fit your roof
  • Installation and wiring by a supervised on-site crew
  • Net-metering application assistance where applicable
  • System handover with a walkthrough of monitoring and basic upkeep
Where the panels come from

Imported from China, on top of a market that already runs on Chinese panels.

Pakistan is one of the largest importers of Chinese-made solar panels in the world, and the panels we bring in for home installs come from that same supply chain. Chinese manufacturers currently supply the large majority of the panels sold in Pakistan, which keeps quality high and pricing competitive compared to importing from anywhere else.
